# Official Links for Coal Mine Audit Details and Checklists

## Purpose

This file contains direct links to official government sources that define audit requirements, inspection schemes and audit checklists for coal mines in India. For each link, it summarises what the source contains.

Use these pages as the authoritative references when building your AI-based governance and compliance monitoring system.

---

## 1. Mandatory Annual Safety Audit (Ministry of Coal)

### Link
- https://coal.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-12/22-12-2023b-wn.pdf

### What it contains
- Ministry of Coal notification directing coal companies/mine owners to strictly adhere to safety-audit guidelines.
- Statement that **safety auditing on an annual basis is mandatory**.
- Requirement that all coal companies/mine owners **submit the audit report to the Coal Controller’s Organisation (CCO) by the end of March every year**.
- Permission for coal companies/mine owners to **hire an external auditor** for this purpose.

## 2. Safety and Health Management Audit Guidelines (Ministry of Coal)

### Link 1 – Annual Report reference
- https://coal.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/chap14AnnualReport2025en2.pdf

### Link 2 – Project proponent safety requirements
- https://www.coal.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-01/31-01-2025a-wn.pdf

### What they contain
- Reference to **Guidelines on “Safety and Health Management Audit in Coal and Lignite Mines”** issued by the Ministry of Coal on **14 December 2023**.
- Requirement that the audit be conducted by **qualified auditors experienced in Safety and Health Management Systems (SHMS)** with domain knowledge.
- Requirement that the **audit report be submitted annually to the office of the Coal Controller** and considered during mining-plan approvals and revisions.
- Project-proponent requirement to prepare a **Safety Management Plan as per Coal Mines Regulations, 2017** and follow Ministry of Coal safety-audit guidelines.

## 3. CIL Internal Safety Audit and Inspection Details

### Link 1 – Safety in Coal Mines (2020 chapter)
- https://coal.gov.in/sites/default/files/2020-09/Chapter11-en.pdf

### Link 2 – Safety in Coal Mines (2026 chapter)
- https://coal.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-02/chap14AnnualReport2026en.pdf

### Link 3 – Safety in Coal Mines (2021–22 chapter)
- https://coal.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-01/annual-report-safety2021-22.pdf

### What they contain
- Description of **annual internal safety audits** of all producing mines of Coal India Limited (CIL).
- Use of **multi-disciplinary Inter-Area Safety Audit teams** of respective subsidiaries.
- **Check Audit** of a sample of mines (e.g. 10%) by **Inter-Subsidiary multidisciplinary teams** to reassess internal audit findings.
- Role of the **Internal Safety Organization (ISO)** in monitoring rectification of deficiencies.
- Details of inspection layers:
  - Daily inspections by unit statutory officials and ISO officials.
  - Workmen Inspectors inspecting various parts of the mine twice a week.
  - Pit Safety Committee (PSC) members inspecting once a month.
  - Inter-Unit Safety Assessment (IUSA) teams inspecting once every three months.
  - Regular and surprise inspections by GM (Safety), corporate ISO officers, senior CIL/subsidiary officials, trade-union representatives and Ministry of Coal officials.
- Emphasis on **Safety Management Plans (SMPs)** prepared through site-specific risk assessment and monitored by ISO.

## 4. DGMS Inspection Scheme

### Link
- https://labour.gov.in/sites/default/files/53bcfba1a9052inspectschemefordgms.pdf

### What it contains
- DGMS inspection scheme under the Ministry of Labour and Employment.
- **Mandatory inspections** in cases of:
  - Fatal accident.
  - Dangerous occurrence.
  - Follow-up of notices issued.
- **Optional, risk-based inspections** considering:
  - Major and operational risks.
  - Mines employing 50% contract labour or at least 20 contract workers.
  - Mines with more than 250 workers.
  - Other mines selected through a computer-generated scheme.
- General instructions for DGMS inspectors:
  - Maintain a list of mines.
  - Ask the Workman Inspector to be present during inspection where applicable.
  - Prepare a **spot inspection report** and obtain the employer representative’s countersignature.
  - Provide a copy of the spot report to the employer.
  - Conduct inspections during normal working hours except in emergencies.
  - Upload the inspection report within **7 days**.

## 5. Star Rating Evaluation Templates (Official Audit Checklists)

### Link 1 – Underground mine template
- https://starrating.coal.gov.in/policy/Template_UG.pdf

### Link 2 – Opencast mine template
- https://starrating.coal.gov.in/policy/Template_OC.pdf

### Link 3 – Star Rating portal
- https://starrating.coal.gov.in/

### Link 4 – Star Rating results
- https://starrating.coal.gov.in/result.php

### What they contain
- **Draft evaluation templates** for underground and opencast coal mines under the Ministry of Coal’s **Star Rating of Coal Mines** programme.
- Detailed **audit checklists** organised into modules such as:
  - Mine geometry / mining operations and OB dumps.
  - Environment (dust suppression, air and water quality, water conservation, energy audit, EMP/EC/FC compliance, reclamation).
  - Technology and best practices (communication systems, mining technologies, ISO certifications, advanced planning/surveying).
  - Economic performance (production/dispatch targets, capacity utilisation, grade slippage, OMS growth, capital budget utilisation).
  - Rehabilitation and resettlement (R&R survey, facilities, employment, skill development, welfare, CSR).
  - Employee/worker compliance (periodic medical examination, sanitation, vocational training, Section-22 orders/notices).
  - Safety and security (safety audit status, ventilation/VEQ, standing orders, roof/pillar stability, man-riding, PSC meetings, accidents/disasters, IT anti-pilferage, safety budget, statutory staff strength, risk-based safety management system, statutory plans, SOPs, gas telemonitoring, Safety Week assessment).
- Scoring rules, multipliers and **star-rating thresholds (0–5 stars)** based on total percentage score.
- These templates can be directly used as the basis for digital audit checklists, scoring engines and risk features.

## How to use these sources in your system

- Store the **source URL, document title, issuing authority, notification date and version** for every audit requirement.
- Map each audit/checklist item to:
  - Frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually, once in 3/5 years).
  - Responsible role (manager, safety officer, ventilation officer, workmen inspector, PSC member, ISO team, external auditor, DGMS inspector).
  - Evidence required (report, inspection book, spot report, photos, sensor logs, minutes, certificates).
  - Submission authority and due date (e.g. CCO by end of March).
- Use the Star Rating templates as the base for:
  - Digital audit forms.
  - Scoring and star-rating calculation.
  - Risk features (overdue audits, repeat non-conformities, safety-budget utilisation, statutory staff strength, accident/disaster counts).
- Implement workflows for:
  - Scheduling and tracking audits and inspections.
  - Geo-tagged, time-stamped mobile checklists.
  - Automated reminders and escalations for overdue items.
  - Role-based dashboards for mine officials, corporate safety, auditors and regulators.
